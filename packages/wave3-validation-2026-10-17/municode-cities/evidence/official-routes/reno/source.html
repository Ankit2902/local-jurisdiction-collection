The Reno City Attorney's Office is located at Reno City Hall, 1 East First Street. Directions.
The State Bar of Nevada offers a free, non-profit, Lawyer Referral and Information Service (LRIS) for the community. If you feel you need an attorney but are not sure who to turn to, LRIS attorneys agree not to charge LRIS clients more than $45 for an initial consultation. The State Bar also offers a series of 13 free brochures, most of which are available in English and Spanish.
The City Clerk's Office maintains, updates, and publishes the Reno Municipal Code (RMC). Copies of the Code are found in most local public libraries and is also available on the Municode website.
Claims for damages against the City must be filed with the City's Risk Manager within the applicable statute of limitations. A claim form can be obtained by calling 775-334-3838. You can also obtain a Claim for Damages form by visiting The Risk Management Division of the City Attorney's Office located in Reno City Hall at 1 East First Street, Reno, NV 89501.
The following organization may be able to help:
Northern Nevada Apartment Association 775-322-6622
Complaints should be directed to Reno Direct by calling 775-334-INFO (775-334-4636) or reporting an issue online. Because other City departments are not supervised by the City Attorney's Office, the City Attorney can typically address complaints only about its own employees.
