WELCOME TO OUR CODES AND REGULATIONS

Lincoln has earned top national rankings for its quality of life and its economy for good reason. This State Capital, college town and hub of the “Silicon Prairie” offers the excitement of a big city with the convenience and friendliness of small town living. Residents are proud of the city’s 131-mile trail network, excellent educational system, low crime rate, high-tech infrastructure and great climate for job seekers and entrepreneurs. Lincoln’s reputation as a welcoming city and its state-of-the-art venues that attract the top entertainment in the world also make it a great place to both live and visit.
