§ 1.07.120. Unlawful acts. 
It shall be unlawful for any Person to willfully prevent or attempt to prevent, by 
intimidation, threats, coercion, or otherwise, any Person from complying with the 
provisions of this chapter. 
(Ord. 27867 Ex. A, 2009-12-15)
