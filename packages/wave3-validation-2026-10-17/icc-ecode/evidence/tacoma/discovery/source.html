    Tacoma Municipal Code (TMC)

    help_center

    The TMC is updated quarterly, and this online version is current through the date listed above.

    • Tips on searching the online code are available in the Help section.  
    • The gavel icon appears next to portions of the code that are scheduled to be updated, due to New Laws.
    • Visit the City’s Legistar website for past and upcoming meeting agendas, minutes and recordings, and legislation.

    Questions? Contact the City Clerk's Office at cityclerk@tacoma.gov or 253-591-5505.
