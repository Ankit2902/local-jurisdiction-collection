§ 203-13. Repealer. 
Except as otherwise provided by §  203-9 of this ordinance, any resolution and ordinance or any 
part thereof conflicting with the provisions of this ordinance is hereby repealed in so far as the 
same affects this ordinance. 
Allegheny County, PA
