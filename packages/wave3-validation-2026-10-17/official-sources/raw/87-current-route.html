Welcome to our Code of Ordinances

Welcome to the City of Savannah GA Code of Ordinances. This tool is designed to make searching and navigating our Code as easy as possible. The City is committed to transparency and this tool will allow our community to quickly explore our code. Please contact the following departments directly or call 311:

  • Clerk of Council’s Office (Code related)
  • Development Services
  • Planning and Urban Design (zoning)
  • Real Estate Services (acquisitions, dispositions, encroachments, surplus property, easements)
  • Traffic Engineering (right-of-way permits)
  • Revenue (utility services, business licensing, alcohol permits)
  • Code Compliance (enforcement)
