Chapter 01.02 General Provisions

Section 01.02.010 Interpretation of Code
  1. Subject to specific definitions and rules of construction as are variously provided in this code, words and phrases used in this code have meaning in accordance with the follow­ing rules:
    1. Terms are defined so as to give effect to the obvious legislative intent.
    1. Particular words are construed in context so as to give meaning to every word used and to achieve consistency within the entire context.
    1. Technical terms and jargon have such special meanings as are generally under­stood by practitioners in the field from custom and usage.
    1. Terms used in laws adopted by reference carry over their meanings in the laws adopted.
    1. Words are given common and ordinary meaning.
  1. The use of nouns and pronouns of the masculine gender is not intended to exclude corresponding words of the feminine gender. Gender, number, and tense are interchangeable.
