SEC. 11.101. TITLE -- REFERENCE TO CODE.
   This code shall be known as the “San Diego County Code.” It shall be sufficient to refer to this code as the “San Diego County Code” in any prosecution for the violation of any provision of the code and it shall also be sufficient to designate any ordinance adding to, amending or repealing the code as an addition or amendment to or repeal of the “San Diego County Code.”
(Amended by Ord. No. 9889 (N.S.), effective 10-26-07)
