Fairfax County, Virginia

Fairfax County Zoning Ordinance

The Zoning Ordinance regulates land use in Fairfax County. It is intended to promote the health, safety, and general welfare of the public and to implement the Comprehensive Plan for the orderly and controlled development of the County. It is administered by the Zoning Administration Division within the Department of Planning and Development whose mission is to promote livable communities which enhance the quality of life for the present and the future. The Zoning Ordinance is subject to periodic revisions upon action by the Board of Supervisors.
