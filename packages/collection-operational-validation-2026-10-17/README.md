## Images

Final collectors emit the exact literal `[IMAGE]` and do not acquire separate image assets, extract embedded image members, decode image pixels, render images or perform OCR. Preserve provision IDs, headings, surrounding legal text, tables and available textual captions. Normalized records and metadata must omit active image markup and image payloads. Browser validation sanitizes source markup in an inert template before attaching it to a document.

Original publisher HTML, PDFs, Word documents and archives can inherently contain images. Their immutable source bytes remain retained; normalized output uses placeholders. A source-proven image-only page may have native text comparison marked not applicable, without claiming transcription. Missing nonimage legal documents, unresolved source references and unexplained text or table differences remain collection gaps.

See `current-runtime/collectors/IMAGE-POLICY.md` and the bound image-policy regression and actual-source audit receipts. Historical audits that attached raw markup before sanitization retain that limitation; current successful receipts do not rewrite their history.
