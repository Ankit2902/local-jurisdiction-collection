Refuse shall mean all garbage as hereinbefore defined, ashes and sweepings, paper, cardboard, rags, glass, feathers, dishes, bottles, crockery, utensils of every kind and nature, pans, pasteboard boxes, lye, poisons, food containers, tin cans, grass cuttings, leaves, tree limbs and branches, discarded automobile bodies, automobiles, parts of automobiles, machinery, parts of machinery, and any other waste matter or material not herein defined as garbage which accumulates in the operation of a household, business establishment, factory, or shop of any kind or nature. (Ord. 15559 §6; May 14, 1990: P.C. §8.44.060: Ord. 11149 §2; July 22, 1974).
