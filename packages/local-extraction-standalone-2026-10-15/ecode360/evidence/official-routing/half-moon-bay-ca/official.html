Municipal Code

Half Moon Bay's Municipal Code sets forth the rules and regulations for a very variety of  issues, from building construction, health and sanitation, and business activities, to overgrown weeds, abandoned vehicles, and many more. Our Municipal Code helps to make our community safe, clean, appealing, and healthy for all.

View the entire Half Moon Bay Municipal Code

Some of the more frequently-visited and useful sections of the Municipal Code are:

  • Title 17 (Subdivisions)
  • Chapter 17.06 (Residential Dwelling Unit Allocation System - Measure D)
  • Title 18 (Zoning)
  • Chapter 7.40 Heritage Trees
  • Chapter 13.04 Landscaping (Water Conservation)
  • Chapter 14.39 Historic Resource Preservation
