For professionals who need more access

Whether reviewing development plans, researching regulations, or advising clients, architects, engineers, real estate professionals, attorneys, and other professionals need more than basic access to municipal codes. An eCode360 Professional Subscription delivers exclusive tools that simplify research, streamline information sharing, and improve productivity, along with access to more than 7,000 municipal codes. All for just $195/year.

Transform the way you work with municipal codes.

Go beyond fundamental code access with exclusive tools that allow you to:

Research More Deeply

Conduct advanced searches (filtered by municipality, geographic region, government type, or size of population) across multiple codes, including archived versions.

Draft More Efficiently

See how different municipalities handle similar issues and use the easy Download to Word feature to amend or draft your own legislation.

Keep Work More Organized

Record personal notes with relevant code sections so important insights are always easy to find.
