Ch. 1.01 Code Adoption | Everett Municipal Code
1.01.010
Adoption.
Pursuant to the provisions of RCW
35.21.500
through
35.21.570
, there is adopted the “Everett Municipal Code,” as compiled, edited and published by Book Publishing Company, Seattle, Washington.
(Ord. 354-75 § 1, 1975.)
The Everett Municipal Code is current through Ordinance 4203-26, passed September 16, 2026.
