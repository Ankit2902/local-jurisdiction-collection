1-1-1 SEAL:

The following be, and is hereby adopted as the Municipal Seal of the City of Renton, State of Washington, to-wit: In the center of said seal the word “SEAL” surrounded by the words “CITY OF RENTON. Incorporated September 6, 1901”. (Ord. 2332, 5-15-67)
