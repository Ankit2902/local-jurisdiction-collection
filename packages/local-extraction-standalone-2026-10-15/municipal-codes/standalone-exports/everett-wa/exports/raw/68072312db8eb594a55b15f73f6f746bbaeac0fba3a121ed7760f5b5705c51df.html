1.01.010 Adoption.

Pursuant to the provisions of RCW 35.21.500 through 35.21.570, there is adopted the “Everett Municipal Code,” as compiled, edited and published by Book Publishing Company, Seattle, Washington. (Ord. 354-75 § 1, 1975.)

The Everett Municipal Code is current through Ordinance 4203-26, passed September 16, 2026.

Disclaimer: The City Clerk’s Office has the official version of the Everett Municipal Code. Users should contact the City Clerk’s Office for ordinances passed subsequent to the ordinance cited above.
