1.01.010 Adoption.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

Pursuant to the provisions of RCW 35.21.500 through 35.21.570, there is adopted the “Everett Municipal Code,” as compiled, edited and published by Book Publishing Company, Seattle, Washington. (Ord. 354-75 § 1, 1975.)

1.01.020 Title—Citation—Reference.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

This code shall be known as the “Everett Municipal Code” and it shall be sufficient to refer to said code as the “Everett Municipal Code” in any prosecution for the violation of any provision thereof or in any proceeding at law or equity. It shall be sufficient to designate any ordinance adding to, amending, correcting or repealing all or any part or portion thereof as an addition to, amendment to, correction or repeal of the Everett Municipal Code. Further reference may be had to the titles, chapters, sections and subsections of the Everett Municipal Code and such references shall apply to that numbered title, chapter, section or subsection as it appears in the code. (Ord. 354-75 § 2, 1975.)

1.01.030 Codification authority.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

This code consists of all the regulatory and penal ordinances and certain of the administrative ordinances of the city of Everett, Washington, codified pursuant to the provisions of RCW 35.21.500 through 35.21.570. (Ord. 354-75 § 3, 1975.)

1.01.040 Ordinances passed prior to adoption of the code.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

The last ordinance included in the initial code is Ordinance No. 317-74. The following ordinances, passed subsequent to Ordinance 317-74, but prior to adoption of this code, are adopted and made a part of this code: Ordinances 318-74 through 350-75. (Ord. 354-75 § 4, 1975.)

1.01.050 Reference applies to all amendments.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

Whenever a reference is made to this code as the “Everett Municipal Code,” or to any portion thereof, or to any ordinance of the city of Everett, Washington, the reference shall apply to all amendments, corrections and additions heretofore, now or hereafter made. (Ord. 354-75 § 5, 1975.)

1.01.060 Title, chapter and section headings.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

Title, chapter and section headings contained herein shall not be deemed to govern, limit, modify or in any manner affect the scope, meaning or intent of the provisions of any title, chapter or section hereof. (Ord. 354-75 § 6, 1975.)

1.01.070 Reference to specific ordinances.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

The provisions of this code shall not in any manner affect matters of record which refer to, or are otherwise connected with ordinances which are therein specifically designated by number or otherwise and which are included within the code, but such reference shall be construed to apply to the corresponding provisions contained within this code. (Ord. 354-75 § 7, 1975.)

1.01.080 Effect of code on past actions and obligations.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

Neither the adoption of this code nor the repeal or amendment hereby of any ordinance or part or portion of any ordinance of the city of Everett shall in any manner affect the prosecution for violations of ordinances, which violations were committed prior to the effective date hereof, nor be construed as a waiver of any license, fee, or penalty at said effective date due and unpaid under such ordinances, nor be construed as affecting any of the provisions of such ordinances relating to the collection of any such license, fee, or penalty, or the penal provisions applicable to any violation thereof, nor to affect the validity of any bond or cash deposit in lieu thereof, required to be posted, filed or deposited pursuant to any ordinance and all rights and obligations thereunder appertaining shall continue in full force and effect. (Ord. 354-75 § 8, 1975.)

1.01.090 Effective date.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

This code shall become effective on the date the ordinance adopting this code as the Everett Municipal Code becomes effective. (Ord. 354-75 § 9, 1975.)

1.01.100 Constitutionality.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

If any section, subsection, sentence, clause or phrase of this code is for any reason held to be invalid or unconstitutional, such decision shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this code. The council declares that it would have passed this code, and each section, subsection, sentence, clause and phrase thereof, irrespective of the fact that any one or more sections, subsections, sentences, clauses or phrases had been declared invalid or unconstitutional, and if for any reason this code should be declared invalid or unconstitutional, then the original ordinance or ordinances shall be in full force and effect. (Ord. 354-75 § 10, 1975.)

1.01.110 Cost.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

Each set of the Everett Municipal Code may be purchased from the city clerk for seventy dollars per set (Vol. 1 and Vol. 2). (Ord. 354-75 § 11, 1975.)

1.01.120 Authority to correct scrivener’s clerical errors.
Search Within This
This section is included in your selections.

The city clerk and the codifiers of ordinances are authorized to make necessary corrections to any ordinance before, during or following codification, including, but not limited to, the correction of scrivener’s clerical errors, references, ordinance numbering, section/subsection numbering and any references thereto. (Ord. 3024-07 § 1, 2007.)

1For statutory provisions authorizing cities to codify their ordinances, see RCW 35.21.500—35.21.570. For the Charter provisions requiring the mayor to cause the annual codification of ordinances, see § 15.6.
