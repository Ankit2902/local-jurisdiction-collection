City of Emeryville, CA
1-1.05. Headings.
The title, chapter and section headings contained in the Emeryville Municipal Code –
1995 Edition shall not be deemed to govern, limit, modify or in any manner affect the
scope, meaning, or intent of the provisions of any title, chapter or section.
(Sec. 2 (part), Ord. 96-006, eff. July 4, 1996)
