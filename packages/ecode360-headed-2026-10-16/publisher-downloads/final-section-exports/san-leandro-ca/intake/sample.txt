               § 1-1-100. DIVISION OF CITY.                                         
               The City of San Leandro is hereby divided into six Council Member Districts. Said
               districts shall be numbered, and the boundaries thereof shall be as indicated on that
                                                                                    
               certain map entitled "City of San Leandro, Alameda County, California, Council
               Member District Map, 2022", a copy of which is attached hereto and incorporated herein
               by reference.
