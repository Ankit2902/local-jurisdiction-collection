    Latest Updates

    Stay informed with the latest UDO news and updates

    • March 26, 2026

      UDO Advisory Committee March Meeting

      The UDO Advisory Committee (UAC) met on Thursday March 26 to discuss potential future amendments to the UDO related to the floodplain and stormwater articles. You can watch the recording of the meeting on the Planning, Design, & Development YouTube Chanel

    • March 23, 2026

      Latest Text Amendment Adopted

      City Council approved text amendment petition #2025-118 on March 23, 2026. This petition was a maintenance text amendment to the Unified Development Ordinance (UDO) that provides greater clarity, updates definitions, updates graphics, adjusts use permissions and prescribed conditions, makes some changes and additions to development standards, updates administrative process language, and corrects scrivener’s errors.
