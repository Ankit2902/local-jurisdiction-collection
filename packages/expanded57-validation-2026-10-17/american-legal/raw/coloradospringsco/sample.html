1.1.101: TITLE:
The ordinances contained in these chapters shall constitute and shall be designated in its entirety the CODE OF THE CITY OF COLORADO SPRINGS 2001, AS AMENDED and may be cited as "the Colorado Springs City Code" or "the City Code" 1 . The adoption of this Code or any of its portions shall not affect the continuing validity of prior ordinances of the City with which this Code is not in conflict. (Ord. 11-18)
