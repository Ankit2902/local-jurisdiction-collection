§ 10.001 TITLE.

   This ordinance, hereinafter also referred to as this Code, shall be known as the Code of Ordinances of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, and may be so cited.
(1957 Rev. Ords., § 16.201; 1992 Code, § 1-1)
Statutory reference:
   Authority to adopt revised ordinances, see SDCL 9-19-15, 9-19-16, 9-19-17
