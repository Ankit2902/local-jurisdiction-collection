1.01.010   Adoption of the update of the Sacramento City Code.
   Subject to the amendments, additions and modifications set forth in the ordinance codified in this chapter, the city code provisions of the document entitled "Sacramento City Code" as compiled, edited and published by the city clerk, with the assistance of the Book Publishing Company, Seattle, Washington, is adopted as an update of the current city code. The document entitled "Sacramento City Code" also includes for reference and convenience the provisions of the Charter of the city of Sacramento. However, the City Charter is a separate and distinct legal document, and is not part of the city code and is not part of the update of the City Code adopted pursuant to the ordinance codified in this chapter. (Ord. 2000-017 § 1)
1.01.020   Ordinances adopted after preparation of the update-Incorporation into city code.
   The last ordinance included in the update prepared by the city clerk, with the assistance of the Book Publishing Company and adopted pursuant to Section 1.01.010, was Ordinance No. 99-034, adopted on June 15, 1999. The following ordinances, passed subsequent to Ordinance No. 99-034, amended, altered or modified provisions of the city code and the comprehensive zoning ordinance as those documents existed prior to adoption of the ordinance codified in this chapter: Ordinances Nos. 99-039,99-043, 99-044, 99-046, 99-049, 99-056, 99-062, 2000-001, 2000-002, and 2000-013.
   It is the intent of the council that the amendments, alterations or modifications resulting from the adoption of the ordinances listed in the previous paragraph shall be given effect and shall be incorporated into and made part of the city code and shall be set forth in the next publication of the code. (Ord. 2000-017 § 2)
