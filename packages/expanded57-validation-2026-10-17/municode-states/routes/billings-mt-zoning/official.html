Zoning Information

Overview

Zoning regulations guide how land and buildings may be used and developed within the City of Billings and certain areas of Yellowstone County. The Planning Division administers zoning regulations, reviews zoning permits and applications, and helps property owners understand zoning requirements.

Short-Term Rentals

  • Airbnb's are welcome in the City of Billings as of 2021.
  • a Short Term Rental Permit and a City Business License are required to operate an Airbnb (any residential rental for stay less than 30 days) in the City limits of Billings.

Zoning Boards & Commissions

Zoning Commission

The  County Zoning Commission reviews zone change requests and special reviews. Their recommendations are forwarded to the County Commissioners for final action.


Board of Adjustment

The County Boards of Adjustment reviews and acts on variances from the Zoning Regulations. This board is quasi-judicial and their decisions are final.
