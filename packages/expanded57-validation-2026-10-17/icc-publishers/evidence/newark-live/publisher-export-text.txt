§ 1:1-7. EFFECT OF REPEAL OF CERTAIN ORDINANCES. [R.O. 1966 § 1:1-7] 
Whenever an ordinance that repeals an earlier ordinance or part thereof is itself repealed, such 
repeal shall not revive the former ordinance or part thereof, unless specific provision is made 
therefor. 
City of Newark, NJ
