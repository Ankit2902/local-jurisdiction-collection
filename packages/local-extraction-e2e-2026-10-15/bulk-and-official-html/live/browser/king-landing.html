King County Code

Explore the laws and charter governing King County.

All permanent or general laws are incorporated into and become a part of the King County Code. The contents of the code establish prima facie the laws of the county that are of a permanent or general nature.

We make the code available for your research use and convenience. If there is an omission or an inconsistency between the code and an ordinance, the ordinance prevails. Failure to codify an ordinance doesn't affect the applicability of the ordinance as law. (K.C.C. 1.02.070). Please notify the Clerk's office if you identify electronic conversion or transmission errors.
