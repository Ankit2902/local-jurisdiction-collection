  - paragraph: The following words and phrases, whenever used in the ordinances of the city, shall be construed as defined in this section unless from the context a different meaning is intended or unless different meaning is specifically defined and more particularly directed to the use of the words or phrases.
  - list:
    - listitem: "\"City\" means the City of Cupertino, California, or the area within the territorial limits of the City of Cupertino, California, and such territory outside of the city over which the city has jurisdiction or control by virtue of any constitutional or statutory provision."
    - listitem: "\"Computation of Time\" means the time within which an act is to be done. It shall be computed by excluding the first day and including the last day; and if the last day is Sunday or a legal holiday, that day shall be excluded."
