Selection and collection notes

  • The 39 California targets already in scope warrant limiting further city expansion there to four top-50 omissions: Fresno, Sacramento, Long Beach and Bakersfield. Los Angeles County and San Diego County add separate county law.
  • New York expands beyond New York City with Buffalo, Rochester, Albany and Westchester County. Washington gains Spokane and Tacoma; Maryland gains Baltimore City in addition to its existing counties.
  • The seven stand-alone county additions are Los Angeles, San Diego, Clark, Westchester, Allegheny, Fairfax and Arlington. County applicability must remain distinct from city applicability.
  • Consolidated or combined city/county targets count once. Honolulu means the City and County of Honolulu, not Urban Honolulu CDP. No separate Davidson County, Jefferson County or Marion County duplicate is added.
  • For each accepted target, inventory the general code, charter, zoning/development code, separately published regulations, and uncodified amendments. Preserve publisher currency dates and supplements. Incorporated model codes require a separate scope decision.
  • Source families are discovery leads, not evidence that a previously tested extractor will work unchanged. Resolve the current publication from the official routing page before qualification.
  • This list does not add further targets in Illinois, Maine, Minnesota or New Mexico because each already has local coverage and the available slots first address absent states and larger omissions. Territories, tribal governments and special districts are outside this recommendation.
